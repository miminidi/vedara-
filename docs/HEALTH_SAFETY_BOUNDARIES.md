# Health Safety Boundaries

This product is wellness/education in GOAL 001, not medicine.

## Allowed in MVP

- general healthy lifestyle content;
- general movement/training explanations;
- general nutrition education;
- habit-building content;
- wellness motivation;
- service cards for consultation request;
- safety note that materials do not replace a specialist.

## Forbidden in MVP

- diagnosis;
- treatment plan;
- medication recommendations;
- insulin/glucose/dose recommendations;
- disease-specific protocols;
- claims like cure, treat, guaranteed result;
- personalized medical conclusions;
- collecting sensitive medical details without a real privacy/legal flow.

## Required safety copy

Use this short note where appropriate:

> Материалы носят информационный характер и не заменяют консультацию специалиста.

For training content, use:

> Перед началом тренировок учитывайте своё состояние и при необходимости проконсультируйтесь со специалистом.

## UI rule

Do not scare users with legal blocks everywhere. Keep the notes short and calm.

## Later stage

If the product evolves into medical/personalized health guidance, create a separate legal/product/security architecture goal before implementation.
