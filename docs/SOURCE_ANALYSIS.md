## Product synthesis

VEDARA is not one simple wellness app. It is an ecosystem with five product layers:

1. **Brand / Ecosystem hub**  
   Explains the philosophy and four directions: Medicine, Longevita, University, Clinic.

2. **Longevita Club**  
   Subscription/community product for women focused on energy, youthfulness, longevity, practices, protocols, group calls and curator support.

3. **Clinic**  
   High-ticket individual programs and consultations: fasting, point of support, health recovery, burnout, rejuvenation, complex states.

4. **University**  
   Education product for future specialists with three stages and formats: video lessons, live sessions, clinical practice and retreats.

5. **App / personal cabinet**  
   Operational layer: progress, practices, lessons, protocols, sessions, products and community entry points.

## MVP decision

The app should not try to deliver all operations for all business lines immediately.

MVP should be a **unified mobile personal cabinet + ecosystem catalog**:

- show all directions;
- route user into club, clinic, university;
- allow mock trial/subscription/access;
- show lessons and progress;
- collect mock leads for clinic/university;
- keep medical/payment/backend logic out of scope until validated.

## Key product risk

Clinic and disease-related programs need legal/medical boundaries. In MVP they must be positioned as information/lead/application flows, not self-treatment protocols.

## Recommended first commercial test

Use the app prototype to sell/validate:

1. Club trial / subscription.
2. Clinic consultation request.
3. University application.

The personal cabinet becomes valuable after the first paid users exist.
