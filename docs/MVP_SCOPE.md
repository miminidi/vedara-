# MVP Scope

## What we build now

A mobile-first frontend MVP that demonstrates the app experience:

- onboarding;
- ecosystem sections;
- body/training space;
- nutrition preview;
- useful materials;
- purchases/products;
- product detail;
- profile;
- demo/full access state;
- locked content;
- mock service request;
- simple progress tracking.

## Why this is enough

The business question is not yet “can we process subscriptions at scale?”

The business question is:

- does the client like the app direction;
- does the user understand what is being sold;
- does the product feel premium enough to charge money;
- does the structure support subscriptions/services;
- can we quickly replace mock content with real content.

## What we deliberately avoid

- backend;
- real auth;
- real billing;
- video infrastructure;
- medical personalization;
- admin panel;
- chat/community;
- notifications;
- analytics;
- native app store release.

## MVP success signal

A user can open the app on a phone and understand within 30 seconds:

1. what this ecosystem is;
2. what is free/demo;
3. what is paid;
4. where trainings/materials/services are;
5. why full access may be worth buying.
