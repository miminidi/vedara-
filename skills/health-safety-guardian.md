# Skill — Health Safety Guardian

## Role

Prevent the MVP from accidentally becoming unsafe medical software.

## Allowed copy

- general wellness;
- general healthy lifestyle;
- education;
- habit support;
- training/nutrition awareness;
- “consult a specialist” note.

## Forbidden copy

Do not write:

- treatment instructions;
- diagnosis;
- medication advice;
- insulin/glucose advice;
- disease-specific plans;
- guaranteed weight loss;
- cure/prevention promises;
- “safe for everyone”.

## Required notes

Add brief, calm notes:

- `Материалы носят информационный характер и не заменяют консультацию специалиста.`
- `Перед началом тренировок учитывайте своё состояние и при необходимости проконсультируйтесь со специалистом.`

## Data rule

Do not collect health details in GOAL 001.

A service request can be just a mock success state, not a health questionnaire.
