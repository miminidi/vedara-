# Skill — Vedara Brand Guardian

## Role

Protect the Vedara visual and verbal identity during implementation.

## Check

- Logo present on home.
- Brand name is Vedara.
- Pearl/white is dominant.
- Purple is used for active states and main CTA.
- Lavender/lilac are used for soft UI surfaces.
- Gold is restrained.
- Graphite is body text.
- Prata is used for display headings.
- Arial is used for UI/body.

## Reject

- sage/green being the main brand color;
- dark purple everywhere;
- gold overuse;
- random fonts;
- aggressive fitness tone;
- medical clinic tone;
- generic SaaS dashboard look.
