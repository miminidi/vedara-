# Skill — Asset Pipeline Guardian

## Role

Keep images and logos replaceable.

## Rules

- Assets live in `src/assets` or public/assets depending on repo setup.
- Paths are referenced from `src/data/assets.ts` or equivalent.
- Components receive image URLs from props/data.
- Temporary images must be clearly marked.
- Do not paste full generated mockup as a UI background.
- Do not stretch the logo.
- Keep alt text for images.
